# Unattended playtest round, 2026-10-14/15

Every feature of Dova Stash was exercised in game by the AutoTest harness (TESTING §9) over 29 homes: the six vanilla
and DLC city homes, the three Hearthfire homes built with cellar and grounds as one home, nine Creation Club homes,
eleven mod-added homes from the player's MO2 "PLAYER HOMES" group (five of them over several cells) and Wynter's
Breezehome Redux. Plans: `tests/ingame/mod/SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash/AutoTest/1N_` to `6N_`; test templates with `cells`:
`.../Templates/AutoTest/`. Runs: `build/autotest/20261014-23*` to `20261015-01*`.

## Fixed in this round (approved 2026-10-15)

- Bug 1 (item protection): `game::SpecialStacks` / `IsDistinctExtraList` (ItemAdapter) narrow the special-copy check
  to lists with distinguishing extras; count-, unique-id- and owner-only lists count as plain (ItemMover uses it
  for the check and for `PutBack`). DESIGN §8.2 and the changelog say so.
- Bug 2 (sell chest tie): `SortPlanner::ResolveDestination` lets a sell chest win an equal-specificity tie over a
  storage container; unit test in `tests/SellTests.cpp`; DESIGN §8.8 and the category rules updated.
The other items below were reported, not changed, in this round; *Closed since* lists the ones fixed later.

## Closed since (2026-10-21)

- Bug 4 (Severin Manor, doc vs code): **Fixed in the docs.** The code was right: TESTING §1.9 and DESIGN §9 now say the

  (dist/Data/SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash/Categories/00_Default.json:289), bound with the gems in every shipped template.

## Potential bugs (plugin)

1. **Fixed** (bug 1 above). **Item protection false positive blocks an item for the session.** `game::ExtraStacks` (ItemAdapter.cpp:492) treats
   every `ExtraDataList` with a count as a "special copy", including lists that only carry `ExtraCount` or ownership
   (how the engine keeps items added next to a container's base-record stock). A plain move from such a container then
   trips the check in ItemMover.cpp:205-237: `[error] Move of plain Salt Pile x1 Large Sack [Skyrim.esm|0x000E15AD] ->
   <player> took a tempered, enchanted, renamed or worn copy; put 0 back. Plain copies ... stay where they are for the
   rest of this session.` (Vlindrel Hall, run 20261015-001554, test 3.8b). Crafting from storage then fell back to Move
   mode ("0 of the 1 Salt Pile needed could be brought"). Any home whose containers stock the same items (vanilla
   alchemy sacks, food barrels) can hit it. Fix: only lists with distinguishing extras (health, enchantment, charge,
   name, worn, soul, poison) are special; same filter in `PutBack`.
2. **Fixed** (bug 2 above). **Sell chest default category never fires in template homes.** *Make sell chest* on a bare container binds
   `misc.clutter` (DESIGN §8.8, HUD "(Clutter)"), but with several containers on one category list order wins
   (DESIGN "Same category on several containers"). Severin Manor's template already binds `misc.clutter` (guest room
   chest 0x36EB9), so Deposit sent tankards and goblets there and the sell chest stayed empty. Most shipped templates
   bind clutter. Suggest the sell chest wins ties for its own categories, or the HUD says the category is taken.
3. **Fixed** (see *Closed since*). **Owner shown as a raw form id.** A faction without a name gives `Dova Stash: not a player home (owned by
   1D02F86F)` (White Lighthouse, WLightHouseFaction; Eligibility.cpp:13-22). Fall back to the EditorID or "a faction".
4. **Fixed in the docs** (see *Closed since*). **Doc/code mismatch, Severin Manor.** The cell is accepted before purchase (faction-owned, location
   `LocTypePlayerHouse`; Eligibility.cpp:198-205) and the containers only carry a warning (line 244). TESTING §1.9 says
   "Before the purchase: not a player home (owned by ...)". One of them is wrong; the Arch-Mage's Quarters (no PlayerHouse
   keyword) are refused as documented.
5. **Fixed** (see *Closed since*). **Home backup write fails under OneDrive.** Twice `Home backup: could not write ...\Backups\<file>.json` right
   after a successful write of the same file. The test machine's `Documents\My Games` is a OneDrive folder (the files
   carry the offline/unpinned cloud attributes, and with the provider stopped they cannot even be deleted):
   `WriteTextFileAtomic`'s rename over a file OneDrive is syncing fails. Many players have Documents in OneDrive.
   Non-fatal by design (only the copy outside Data); a short retry, or falling back to writing the file in place, would
   make it stick.
6. **Fixed** (see *Closed since*). **Editor vs plugin disagree on respawning containers in never-resetting cells.** The plugin accepts them ("has the
   respawn flag; accepted because the cell never resets", Controller.cpp:1000); the editor's drafts mark them ignored
   ("Respawns: what you store here can disappear") in Bal Ruhn, Sicarius' Refuge, Black Fur Refuge, Ebongrove and
   Wynter's cell. One rule for both.

## Potential bugs (editor / data / tools)

- **Fixed.** Home scan missed **Myrwatch**: both tower cells share one location; `editor/src/home/Drafts.cpp:211`
  made the higher-scoring gallery the group's main cell, unlike the shipped template. USCCCP's override of the tower
  cell drops its interior flag, name and location; the scan now treats every CELL-group cell as an interior, takes the
  first plugin's name, and lists every cell a template names (`home::TemplateCellsOf`), with the template's main cell
  first. Covered by an EditorTests fixture and `editor_golden.py`'s home-list step.
- **Fixed.** Home scan missed **White Lighthouse**: `editor/src/home/Cell.cpp:489` treated any non-player owner as
  "other"; the plugin accepts a faction the player is in (Eligibility.cpp:66-75). A cell owned by a faction its own
  mod adds (the one the player joins with the home) is now a likely home, and that faction's references are nobody
  else's (`Cell.cpp` `ownedByHomeFaction`).
- **Fixed (editor drafts)** for the first two points: drafts leave out containers that start disabled with no enable
  parent and scripted weapon mounts (Wynter_WeaponMountPoint 0x811B43). Wynter's four *initially disabled* containers
  have an enable parent; they stay listed as *appears later* and are never the master.
  **Fixed (data):** Winter Cove (template 1.1.0): the master is now the main-hall chest `0x23D65`, and the storage
  chest `0x216D8` is the storage room's own area master (in-game check: plan 44, 2.3b-WinterCove and 3.9c-WinterCove). Tundra Homestead's
  merged template gives its cellar an area master (`0xF85`), so a Deposit there leaves nothing with the player (plan
  38). **By design:** Antennaria's master `0xB018` is in the main hall already; the Library has no containers, so it
  cannot have a catch-all (checker info `areasWithoutContainers`), and a Deposit there leaves homeless items with the
  player. **By design:** Shadowfoot's template lists the Creation Club's developer test container as ignored
  (*Test items (by the weapon rack cupboard)*, note *Developer test container.*), so it is never used.
- **Fixed** (see *Closed since*). Categories: the Curios **Welkynd Stone** counted as `alchemy.potions` (Restock
  handed it out for a potion quota).
- **By design (tools).** `esm_dump.py` `persistent` reads the record flag only, so it under-reports (the engine
  promotes scripted/linked refs: Tel Jerdein's Root Cellar dresser). It errs on the safe side: such a container is
  reported as reachable only in its own cell, and the template gives each area a master of its own (Tel Jerdein
  1.1.0). New this round: `cell` lists `furniture` (with crafting keywords and enable parents) and `doors` (with the
  real destination grid cell).

## Design notes worth a look

- A persistent exterior container is out of reach ("not loaded here (its area has not been visited this session)")
  until the player has been in its area once per session. Addressed: Deposit's HUD line now says why the items
  stay ("3 items left: visit Grounds first", DESIGN §6.7); plan 21 3.9.1-unvisited passes (run 20261028-013814).
- For 750 ms after Dova Stash's own moves container events are ignored (ItemMover grace): a hand placement in that
  window is neither sorted on arrival nor learned. Unreachable for a human player.
- The hotkey's template-offer box and the hotkey menu (`iMode = 1`) are message boxes: left open they block every
  container and crafting menu (a player closes them; the harness now fails the test that leaves one open).
- A nameless item (Skyrim.esm|0x0010FCFB) left the player's inventory during a smelter session without a Dova Stash
  move: probably a game token. It is `LItemBarrelFoodSame70`, a barrel's leveled list (LVLI) the engine resolves into
food; snapshots now leave out leveled lists on every plan (TESTING §9, `Steps.cpp` `IsEngineToken`).

their test templates (`"templates"`); `conserved` counts the home's other cells; `store`/`take` wait out the move
