[size=4][i]Look at a chest, press [b]K[/b], and your haul goes where it belongs.[/i][/size]

Auto-sorting storage for any player home. One DLL for SE, AE and VR, no ESP.[/center]

[line]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-01-features.png[/img][/center]

[b]Dova Stash[/b] puts your loot away for you: weapons to the armory, ingredients to the alchemy corner, everything else to the master chest. Popular homes come with ready-made setups. Anywhere else, pick a master chest and put each item away once. Dova Stash remembers where it goes.

[list]
[*][b]Any home.[/b] Vanilla, Hearthfire, Creation Club or mod-added. There's no whitelist: a home qualifies when you own it, it's a player house, or nobody owns it.
[*][b]A master container as your inbox.[/b] [b]Deposit[/b] sends everything you aren't keeping to where it belongs. Items you drop into the master are sorted out of it right away.
[*][b]37 keyword-driven categories.[/b] Weapons by type, armor by weight class, potions, ingredients, books, soul gems, ores, gems and more, defined in JSON. Items that KID gives vendor keywords sort without a patch.
[*][b]Learns as you go.[/b] Put an item into a container by hand once and that container becomes its home. Enchanted variants count as the item they are made from. Changed your mind? Press [b]K[/b] within a few seconds to undo. After three items of one kind, Dova Stash asks once: "Send all Books to End Table?"
[*][b]Fills your displays.[/b] Press [b]K[/b] at a weapon rack, mannequin, bookshelf or plaque to use it, and pick what it takes, like a container: a rack for bows, a mannequin for light armor, a shelf for skill books. Your best weapons go up on the racks, your books on the shelves (as many as each holds, never more), and dragon priest masks, claws and Elder Scrolls on the plaques made for them, as if you had placed them by hand. Nothing is taken down. The Menu Framework's [b]Displays[/b] page lists every display in the home and can use all the racks at once.
[*][b]Dresses your mannequins.[/b] Each one wears your best matching armor set, or the set you pick from a searchable list of every set in your load order, modded sets included.
[*][b]Materials at the workbench.[/b] At a home's forge, smelter, tanning rack, cooking pot, alchemy lab or enchanter, the materials stored in the home come to you, and what you don't use goes back when you're done. What you just made stays with you. Modded recipes and materials work too.
[*][b]A sell chest for your junk.[/b] Make any container the sell chest: Clutter goes there, plus whatever you put in by hand. Nothing is sold until you choose [b]Sell now[/b] and confirm the total ("Sell 14 items for 862 gold?"), at the price a merchant would pay you. Stolen, improved, quest and favorited items, potions you made and what your keep rules keep never sell, and [b]Buy back[/b] undoes your last sale. Optional: sell by itself after a number of game hours.
[*][b]Homes over several areas.[/b] Add the cellar or the grounds outside to a home: walk out of it and press [b]K[/b]. The forge outside then brings what is stored inside, and Deposit works in every area. Your existing setups don't change.
[*][b]22 ready-made templates[/b] for vanilla, Hearthfire, Dragonborn and Creation Club homes. Save your own from the hotkey menu, learned items included, to share or to restore after a reset.
[*][b]Your setups are backed up.[/b] Every change is also copied outside the Data folder, where cleaning MO2's overwrite folder can't reach it. Walk into a home whose setup is gone and press [b]K[/b]: [b]Restore your last setup[/b] brings it back.
[*][b]Never loses an item.[/b] Enchantments, tempering, custom names, stolen flags and souls survive every move. Equipped, favorited and quest items stay with you. Containers that empty themselves when the area resets are refused. The only items that ever leave the game are the ones you sell from the sell chest, after you confirm.
[*][b]Your UI, not ours.[/b] In-world menus are UIExtensions lists (any skin), with the game's message boxes as the fallback. Settings and the full editor live in SKSE Menu Framework.
[*][b]QuickLoot IE support.[/b] A container's categories show as your own SkyUI icons, and items that belong elsewhere read "Iron Sword > Weapon Rack".
[*][b]Live settings.[/b] Every option changes in game without a restart.
[/list]

[b]Setting up a home[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Homes with a template:[/b] walk in and you're told a setup is available. Press [b]K[/b] at any container to turn it on.
[*][b]Any other home:[/b] look at a large chest that is always there, press [b]K[/b] and make it the [b]master[/b].
[*]Then press [b]K[/b] at each other container and pick its [b]Categories...[/b], or skip that and put items away by hand once. Either way, the container becomes their home.
[*]Mark anything you never want used (a barrel that respawns, say) as [b]Ignore[/b]: it stays out for good. [b]Remove[/b] just forgets a container you set up by mistake.
[*]Forge outside, chests inside? Walk out of the door and press [b]K[/b]: [b]Add this area to Lakeview Manor[/b], and give it a name ("Grounds"). Chests in another area are used when the game keeps them in memory (most Hearthfire chests, wardrobes and safes); sacks and barrels only from their own area.
[/list]

[b]Day to day[/b]
[list]
[*]Come home, press [b]K[/b] and choose [b]Deposit[/b]. Everything you aren't keeping goes to its container. Items with no destination yet wait in the master.
[*]Drop things into the master and they leave it for their containers right away, or when you close it.
[*][b]Retrieve...[/b] at the master brings a category back out, or everything.
[*][b]Sort all[/b] re-sorts the master and every container after you change what goes where.
[/list]

[b]What you see in game[/b]
[list]
[*]The activate prompt names a container's role: "Chest (Weapons, Armor)", or what it has learned: "End Table (Books, learned)".
[*]With QuickLoot IE, the master reads "Master", other containers show their category icons, and a [b]Stash[/b] button shows your hotkey.
[*][i]Optional:[/i] floating category icons above the containers around you. Off by default, not on VR.
[*]You can rename any container, and restore its original name later.
[/list]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-03-templates.png[/img][/center]

Homes with a template need no setup: they turn on as soon as you walk in. Set [font=Courier New]bAutoEnableTemplates=false[/font] to be asked first.

[list]
[*][b]Vanilla and DLC:[/b] Breezehome, Honeyside, Hjerim, Proudspire Manor, Vlindrel Hall, Severin Manor, and Lakeview Manor, Heljarchen Hall and Windstad Manor with their cellars.
[*][b]Creation Club:[/b] Bloodchill Manor, Gallows Hall, Goldenhills Plantation, Hendraheim, Myrwatch and its gallery, Nchuanthumz, Shadowfoot Sanctum, Tundra Homestead and its cellar. Each is offered only if you own that content.
[/list]
Applying a template copies it into your own setup, so your edits are never overwritten. Re-applying replaces only the master and the category bindings, and writes a backup first. A home with several templates lets you switch between them in the SKSE Menu Framework (This home, or the Templates page): your learned items and keep rules stay, nothing leaves its container, and a template someone made always comes before one the editor drafted in bulk.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-04-requirements.png[/img][/center]

[list]
[*][url=https://skse.silverlock.org/]SKSE64[/url] for your game version (or SKSEVR for VR)
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32444]Address Library for SKSE Plugins[/url] (or VR Address Library for SKSEVR)
[*][i]Optional:[/i] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/120352]SKSE Menu Framework[/url] for the settings page and the full editor. Without it, settings are in an INI.
[*][i]Optional:[/i] UIExtensions for the in-world list menus. Without it, the game's message boxes are used.
[*][i]Optional:[/i] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/120075]QuickLoot IE[/url]
[/list]
Not required: PapyrusUtil, KID, or any patch.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-05-installation.png[/img][/center]

[b]New install:[/b] install with your mod manager and launch through SKSE. There is no ESP, ESL or ESM, so it takes no load order slot.

[b]MO2:[/b] your home setups are written to [font=Courier New]SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash/Layouts/[/font], which MO2 puts in [b]overwrite[/b]. Move them into their own mod once your homes are set up. A copy of each is also kept in [font=Courier New]Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE/DovaStash/Backups/[/font], which MO2 doesn't touch, so cleaning overwrite doesn't lose them: the hotkey offers [b]Restore your last setup[/b].

[b]Updating:[/b] install over the old version. Your setups are separate files and are kept.

[b]Uninstalling:[/b] remove it any time, mid-playthrough too. Your items stay in the containers they were sorted into.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-06-configuration.png[/img][/center]

Open [b]SKSE Menu Framework[/b] and go to [b]Dova Stash[/b]. Changes apply right away. Hover any option to see its INI key.
[list]
[*][b]Settings (all homes):[/b]
[list]
[*]Hotkeys, keyboard and gamepad
[*]What the hotkey opens: UIExtensions lists, message boxes or the panel
[*]Sort on arrival, sort when the master closes, learning (undo time, when to suggest a category), filling displays
[*]Enable templates automatically
[*]Sell chest: price, and selling by itself after a number of game hours (off)
[*]QuickLoot IE: item tags and category icons
[*]Activate prompt and floating icons
[*]Log detail
[*]Keep rules for every home
[/list]
[*][b]This home:[/b] actions, the sell chest and this session's sales, switching between the home's templates, containers and their categories, learned items, this home's keep rules, saving the home as a template, and restoring a saved copy of its setup.
[/list]
[b]Keep rules[/b] decide what Deposit leaves in your inventory: equipped, favorited and quest items, gold, lockpicks and torches by default.

No Menu Framework? Every option is in [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash.ini[/font], with comments.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-07-compatibility.png[/img][/center]

[list]
[*][b]Game versions:[/b] one DLL for SE 1.5.97, AE 1.6.x and VR. In-game testing so far has been on AE 1.6.1170. VR is experimental (message boxes only, keyboard hotkey).
[*][b]Skyrim Souls:[/b] supported. Items dropped into the master are sorted while its menu is still open.
[*][b]UI skins:[/b] Dova Stash uses each UI mod's own interface, never a particular skin, so any skin for UIExtensions, QuickLoot IE or SkyUI works.
[*][b]Scripted containers[/b] (bookshelves, display cases) are never learned automatically.
[*][b]Homes that sort by themselves[/b] (Clockwork, Legacy of the Dragonborn): Dova Stash leaves them alone, so the two never move the same items.
[*][b]Other large home mods:[/b] no templates yet. Setting one up by hand takes a minute.
[/list]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-08-template-authoring.png[/img][/center]

A template is a small JSON file that says which container in a home holds which categories, and which racks, mannequins and bookshelves are filled with what, down to one weapon or armor set, even from other mods (a player without that mod still gets the rest of the template). Set a home up in game and choose [b]Save as template...[/b] in the hotkey menu (or the panel's Template tab), or use [font=Courier New]tools/esm_dump.py[/font] from the [url=https://github.com/Kexodus/DovaStash]source repository[/url] to find the containers and validate your file. The [url=https://github.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/blob/main/tools/README.md]template guide[/url] walks through it. Good templates for popular homes may be bundled, with credit.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-09-faq.png[/img][/center]

[list]
[*][b]Why didn't an item move?[/b] A keep rule kept it. Or it has no destination yet and waits in the master. Or a tempered or enchanted copy of it stays behind, and the plain copies stay with it rather than risk moving the wrong one.
[*][b]Where are my homes saved?[/b] [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash/Layouts/[/font], one file per home, outside your save, with a copy in [font=Courier New]Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE/DovaStash/Backups/[/font] (plus the copies from your last 5 sessions). A home you set up once works for every character.
[*][b]Can I share or back up my setup?[/b] Yes: [b]Save as template...[/b] in the hotkey menu writes a template, with your learned items as fixed item bindings (untick [b]Keep learned items[/b] in the panel's Template tab to share one without them). The home offers it at once, so after a reset [b]Use template[/b] brings the setup back. Setups are also backed up by themselves (see above).
[/list]

Found a bug? Please include:
[list]
[*]Your Skyrim version (SE, AE or VR) and store (Steam or GOG)
[*][font=Courier New]Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE/DovaStash.log[/font]
[*]Which home and container it happened with, and any home or UI mods you use
[/list]
For sorting problems, set the log detail to [i]Trace[/i] (Diagnostics on the Menu Framework settings page, or [font=Courier New]iLogLevel = 0[/font] in [font=Courier New]DovaStash.ini[/font]), reproduce the problem, and attach the log. Bug reports go here or in [url=https://github.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/issues]GitHub Issues[/url].
