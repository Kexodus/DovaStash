[size=4][i]Look at a chest, press [b]K[/b], and your haul goes where it belongs.[/i][/size]

Auto-sorting storage for any player home. One DLL for SE, AE and VR, no ESP.[/center]

[line]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-01-features.png[/img][/center]

[b]Dova Stash[/b] puts your loot away for you: weapons to the armory, ingredients to the alchemy corner, everything else to the master chest. Popular homes come with ready-made layouts. Anywhere else, pick a master chest and put each item away once. Dova Stash remembers where it goes.

[list]
[*][b]Any home.[/b] Vanilla, Hearthfire, Creation Club or mod-added. There's no whitelist: a home qualifies when you own it, it's a player house, or nobody owns it.
[*][b]A master container as your inbox.[/b] [b]Deposit[/b] sends everything you aren't keeping to where it belongs. Items you drop into the master are sorted out of it right away.
[*][b]37 keyword-driven categories.[/b] Weapons by type, armor by weight class, potions, ingredients, books, soul gems, ores, gems and more, defined in JSON. Items that KID gives vendor keywords sort without a patch.
[*][b]Learns as you go.[/b] Put an item into a container by hand once and that container becomes its home.
[*][b]22 ready-made templates[/b] for vanilla, Hearthfire, Dragonborn and Creation Club homes. Export your own from the game.
[*][b]Never loses an item.[/b] Enchantments, tempering, custom names, stolen flags and souls survive every move. Equipped, favorited and quest items stay with you. Containers that empty themselves when the area resets are refused.
[*][b]Your UI, not ours.[/b] In-world menus are UIExtensions lists (any skin), with the game's message boxes as the fallback. Settings and the full editor live in SKSE Menu Framework.
[*][b]QuickLoot IE support.[/b] A container's categories show as your own SkyUI icons, and items that belong elsewhere read "Iron Sword > Weapon Rack".
[*][b]Live settings.[/b] Every option changes in game without a restart.
[/list]

[b]Setting up a home[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Homes with a template:[/b] walk in and you're told a layout is available. Press [b]K[/b] at any container to turn it on.
[*][b]Any other home:[/b] look at a large chest that is always there, press [b]K[/b] and make it the [b]master[/b].
[*]Then press [b]K[/b] at each other container and pick its [b]Categories...[/b], or skip that and put items away by hand once. Either way, the container becomes their home.
[*]Mark anything you never want used (display cases, bookshelves) as [b]Ignore[/b].
[/list]

[b]Day to day[/b]
[list]
[*]Come home, press [b]K[/b] and choose [b]Deposit[/b]. Everything you aren't keeping goes to its container. Items with no destination yet wait in the master.
[*]Drop things into the master and they leave it for their containers right away, or when you close it.
[*][b]Retrieve...[/b] at the master brings a category back out, or everything.
[*][b]Sort all[/b] re-sorts the master and every container after you change what goes where.
[/list]

[b]What you see in game[/b]
[list]
[*]The activate prompt names a container's role: "Chest (Weapons, Armor)".
[*]With QuickLoot IE, the master reads "Master", other containers show their category icons, and a [b]Stash[/b] button shows your hotkey.
[*][i]Optional:[/i] floating category icons above the containers around you. Off by default, not on VR.
[*]You can rename any container, and restore its original name later.
[/list]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-03-templates.png[/img][/center]

Homes with a template need no setup. Set [font=Courier New]bAutoEnableTemplates=true[/font] and they turn on as soon as you walk in.

[list]
[*][b]Vanilla and DLC:[/b] Breezehome, Honeyside, Hjerim, Proudspire Manor, Vlindrel Hall, Severin Manor, and Lakeview Manor, Heljarchen Hall and Windstad Manor with their cellars.
[*][b]Creation Club:[/b] Bloodchill Manor, Gallows Hall, Goldenhills Plantation, Hendraheim, Myrwatch and its gallery, Nchuanthumz, Shadowfoot Sanctum, Tundra Homestead and its cellar. Each is offered only if you own that content.
[/list]
Applying a template copies it into your own layout, so your edits are never overwritten. Re-applying replaces only the category bindings and writes a backup first.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-04-requirements.png[/img][/center]

[list]
[*][url=https://skse.silverlock.org/]SKSE64[/url] for your game version (or SKSEVR for VR)
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32444]Address Library for SKSE Plugins[/url] (or VR Address Library for SKSEVR)
[*][i]Optional:[/i] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/120352]SKSE Menu Framework[/url] for the settings page and the full editor. Without it, settings are in an INI.
[*][i]Optional:[/i] UIExtensions for the in-world list menus. Without it, the game's message boxes are used.
[*][i]Optional:[/i] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/120075]QuickLoot IE[/url]
[/list]
Not required: PapyrusUtil, KID, or any patch.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-05-installation.png[/img][/center]

[b]New install:[/b] install with your mod manager and launch through SKSE. There is no ESP, ESL or ESM, so it takes no load order slot.

[b]MO2:[/b] your home layouts are written to [font=Courier New]SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash/Layouts/[/font], which MO2 puts in [b]overwrite[/b]. Move them into their own mod once your homes are set up.

[b]Updating:[/b] install over the old version. Your layouts are separate files and are kept.

[b]Uninstalling:[/b] remove it any time, mid-playthrough too. Your items stay in the containers they were sorted into.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-06-configuration.png[/img][/center]

Open [b]SKSE Menu Framework[/b] and go to [b]Dova Stash[/b]. Changes apply right away. Hover any option to see its INI key.
[list]
[*][b]Settings (all homes):[/b]
[list]
[*]Hotkeys, keyboard and gamepad
[*]What the hotkey opens: UIExtensions lists, message boxes or the panel
[*]Sort on arrival, sort when the master closes, learning
[*]Enable templates automatically
[*]QuickLoot IE: item tags and category icons
[*]Activate prompt and floating icons
[*]Log detail
[*]Keep rules for every home
[/list]
[*][b]This home:[/b] actions, containers and their categories, learned items, this home's keep rules, and template export.
[/list]
[b]Keep rules[/b] decide what Deposit leaves in your inventory: equipped, favorited and quest items, gold, lockpicks and torches by default.

No Menu Framework? Every option is in [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash.ini[/font], with comments.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-07-compatibility.png[/img][/center]

[list]
[*][b]Game versions:[/b] one DLL for SE 1.5.97, AE 1.6.x and VR. In-game testing so far has been on AE 1.6.1170. VR is experimental (message boxes only, keyboard hotkey).
[*][b]Skyrim Souls:[/b] supported. Items dropped into the master are sorted while its menu is still open.
[*][b]UI skins:[/b] Dova Stash uses each UI mod's own interface, never a particular skin, so any skin for UIExtensions, QuickLoot IE or SkyUI works.
[*][b]Scripted containers[/b] (bookshelves, display cases) are never learned automatically.
[*][b]Large home mods[/b] (Legacy of the Dragonborn and others): no templates yet. Setting one up by hand takes a minute. Leave display containers alone.
[/list]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-08-template-authoring.png[/img][/center]

A template is a small JSON file that says which container in a home holds which categories. Set a home up in game and press [b]Export[/b] on the panel's Template tab, or use [font=Courier New]tools/esm_dump.py[/font] from the [url=https://github.com/Kexodus/DovaStash]source repository[/url] to find the containers and validate your file. The [url=https://github.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/blob/main/tools/README.md]template guide[/url] walks through it. Good templates for popular homes may be bundled, with credit.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-09-faq.png[/img][/center]

[list]
[*][b]Why didn't an item move?[/b] A keep rule kept it. Or it has no destination yet and waits in the master. Or a tempered or enchanted copy of it stays behind, and the plain copies stay with it rather than risk moving the wrong one.
[*][b]Where are my homes saved?[/b] [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash/Layouts/[/font], one file per home, outside your save. A home you set up once works for every character.
[*][b]Can I share my setup?[/b] Yes: [b]Export[/b] in the editor's Template tab writes a shareable template without your personal learned items.
[/list]

Found a bug? Please include:
[list]
[*]Your Skyrim version (SE, AE or VR) and store (Steam or GOG)
[*][font=Courier New]Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE/DovaStash.log[/font]
[*]Which home and container it happened with, and any home or UI mods you use
[/list]
For sorting problems, set the log detail to [i]Trace[/i] (Diagnostics on the Menu Framework settings page, or [font=Courier New]iLogLevel = 0[/font] in [font=Courier New]DovaStash.ini[/font]), reproduce the problem, and attach the log. Bug reports go here or in [url=https://github.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/issues]GitHub Issues[/url].
