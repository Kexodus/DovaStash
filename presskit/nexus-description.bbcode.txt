[size=4]Auto-sorting storage for any player home.[/size][/center]

Look at a chest, press [b]K[/b], and your haul goes where it belongs: weapons to the armory, ingredients to the alchemy corner, everything else to the master chest. Popular homes come with ready-made layouts. Anywhere else, pick a master chest and put each item away once. Dova Stash remembers where it goes.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/dovastash-gallery-how-it-works-1920x1080.png[/img][/center]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-01-features.png[/img]

[list]
[*][b]Any home[/b]: vanilla, Hearthfire, Creation Club or mod-added. No whitelist. A home qualifies when you own it, it's a player house, or nobody owns it.
[*][b]Master container[/b]: your loot's inbox. [b]Deposit[/b] sends everything you aren't keeping to where it belongs. Items you drop into the master are sorted out of it right away.
[*][b]37 keyword-driven categories[/b]: weapons by type, armor by weight class, potions, ingredients, books, soul gems, ores, gems and more, defined in JSON. Items that KID gives vendor keywords sort without a patch.
[*][b]Learns as you go[/b]: put an item into a container by hand once and that container becomes its home.
[*][b]22 ready-made templates[/b] for vanilla, Hearthfire, Dragonborn and Creation Club homes. Export your own from the game.
[*][b]Never loses an item[/b]: enchantments, tempering, custom names, stolen flags and souls survive every move. Equipped, favorited and quest items stay with you. Containers that empty themselves when the area resets are refused.
[*][b]Your UI, not ours[/b]: in-world menus are UIExtensions lists (any skin), with the game's message boxes as the fallback. Settings and the full editor live in SKSE Menu Framework.
[*][b]QuickLoot IE[/b]: a container's categories show as your own SkyUI icons, and items that belong elsewhere read "Iron Sword > Weapon Rack".
[*][b]Live settings[/b]: every option changes in game without a restart.
[/list]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/dovastash-features-1200x700.png[/img][/center]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-03-templates.png[/img]

Homes with a template need no setup. Walk in, press [b]K[/b] at any container to turn it on, then Deposit. Set [font=Courier New]bAutoEnableTemplates=true[/font] to skip even that.

[list]
[*][b]Vanilla and DLC[/b]: Breezehome, Honeyside, Hjerim, Proudspire Manor, Vlindrel Hall, Severin Manor, and Lakeview Manor, Heljarchen Hall and Windstad Manor with their cellars.
[*][b]Creation Club[/b]: Bloodchill Manor, Gallows Hall, Goldenhills Plantation, Hendraheim, Myrwatch and its gallery, Nchuanthumz, Shadowfoot Sanctum, Tundra Homestead and its cellar. Each is offered only if you own that content.
[/list]

Applying a template copies it into your own layout, so your edits are never overwritten. Re-applying replaces only the category bindings and writes a backup first.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-05-installation.png[/img]

[list=1]
[*]Install the main file with Mod Organizer 2, Vortex, or any mod manager.
[*]Launch through SKSE.
[/list]

There is no ESP, ESL or ESM, so it takes no load order slot. You can add or remove it mid-playthrough: items stay where they are.

[b]MO2:[/b] your home layouts are written to [font=Courier New]SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash/Layouts/[/font], which MO2 puts in [b]overwrite[/b]. Move them into their own mod once your homes are set up.

[b]Updating:[/b] install over the old version. Your layouts are separate files and are kept.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-06-configuration.png[/img]

[list]
[*][b]In game:[/b] SKSE Menu Framework → [i]Dova Stash / Settings (all homes)[/i]. Changes apply right away. Hover any option to see its INI key.
[*][b]INI:[/b] [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash.ini[/font]. Keys you leave out use their defaults.
[*][b]Keep rules[/b] decide what Deposit leaves in your inventory: equipped, favorited and quest items, gold, lockpicks and torches by default. Set them per home or for every home.
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-07-compatibility.png[/img]

[list]
[*][b]Game versions:[/b] one DLL for SE 1.5.97, AE 1.6.x and VR. In-game testing so far has been on AE 1.6.1170. VR is experimental (message boxes only, keyboard hotkey).
[*][b]Skyrim Souls:[/b] supported. Items dropped into the master are sorted while its menu is still open.
[*][b]Scripted containers[/b] (bookshelves, display cases) are never learned automatically.
[*][b]Large home mods[/b] (Legacy of the Dragonborn and others): no templates yet. Setting one up by hand takes a minute. Leave display containers alone.
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-08-template-authoring.png[/img]

A template is a small JSON file that says which container in a home holds which categories. Use [font=Courier New]tools/esm_dump.py[/font] from the [url=https://github.com/Kexodus/DovaStash]source repository[/url] to find the containers and validate your file. The [url=https://github.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/blob/main/tools/README.md]template guide[/url] walks through it. Good templates for popular homes may be bundled, with credit.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/DovaStash/main/presskit/nexus/section-09-faq.png[/img]

[list]
[*][b]Why didn't an item move?[/b] A keep rule kept it. Or it has no destination yet and waits in the master. Or a tempered or enchanted copy of it stays behind, and the plain copies stay with it rather than risk moving the wrong one.
[*][b]Where are my homes saved?[/b] [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash/Layouts/[/font], one file per home, outside your save. A home you set up once works for every character.
[*][b]Can I share my setup?[/b] Yes: [b]Export[/b] in the editor's Template tab writes a shareable template without your personal learned items.
[/list]
