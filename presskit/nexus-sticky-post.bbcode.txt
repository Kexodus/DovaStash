[b]Bug reports: please read before posting[/b]

Thanks for trying Dova Stash. It's a first release, and a good report usually gets a fix quickly. Please include the following:

[list=1]
[*][b]Your log file[/b], [i]DovaStash.log[/i]:
[list]
[*]SE / AE: [i]Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE/DovaStash.log[/i]
[*]VR: [i]Documents/My Games/Skyrim VR/SKSE/DovaStash.log[/i]
[/list]
For the most useful log, set [b]iLogLevel = 1[/b] in [i]Data/SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash.ini[/i], restart, and reproduce the problem. Upload the log to a paste site (Pastebin, GitHub Gist) and post the link. Please don't paste the whole log into a comment.
[*][b]Game version[/b] (SE 1.5.97, AE 1.6.x with the exact number, or VR) and your [b]SKSE version[/b].
[*][b]The home[/b]: its name, and the mod it comes from if it isn't vanilla. Say whether you used a template or set it up by hand.
[*][b]UI[/b]: SKSE Menu Framework panel or message boxes.
[*][b]Steps[/b]: what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
[*][b]For missing or misplaced items[/b]: the item's name, where it was before, and where you expected it to go. If you can, attach your layout file from [i]Data/SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash/Layouts/[/i] (MO2: the [b]overwrite[/b] folder).
[/list]

[b]Before posting, please check these[/b]
[list]
[*]An item that didn't move is usually a keep rule (equipped, favorited, quest, gold, lockpicks, torches), an item with no destination (it stays in the master), or a spare copy of an equipped item. See [i]Why didn't item X move?[/i] in the FAQ.
[*]A [i]"not a player home"[/i] message means the cell is owned by someone else and isn't tagged as a player house.
[*][b]VR players:[/b] VR support is experimental, and any report helps, including "it works".
[/list]

[b]Templates:[/b] if you've made a template for a home, post a link here or upload it as its own file. Good ones may be bundled, with credit.
