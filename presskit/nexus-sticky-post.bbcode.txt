Thanks for trying Dova Stash. It's a pre-release, and a good report usually gets a fix quickly. Please include the following:

[list=1]
[*][b]Your log file[/b], [i]DovaStash.log[/i]:
[list]
[*]SE / AE: [i]Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE/DovaStash.log[/i]

For the most useful log, set the log detail to [b]Trace[/b] (Settings page, Log) or [b]iLogLevel = 0[/b] in [i]Data/SKSE/Plugins/DovaStash.ini[/i], then reproduce the problem (no restart needed). Upload the log to a paste site (Pastebin, GitHub Gist) and post the link. Please don't paste the whole log into a comment.

[*][b]Steps[/b]: what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

[b]Before posting, please check these[/b]
[list]
[*]An item that didn't move is usually a keep rule (equipped, favorited, quest, gold, lockpicks, torches), an item with no destination (it stays in the master; in a home over several areas, it stays with you while no master is in reach: "master not here"; "visit Cellar first" means its container is in an area you haven't been to this session; "no container here takes them" means its container is in an area that isn't loaded: Deposit there, or give a container here that category), an item a script hands back (the Curators Storage Token), or, rarely, an item Dova Stash holds back for the session after the game once picked a tempered or worn copy of it (the log says so). See [i]Why didn't an item move?[/i] in the FAQ.
[*]A [i]"not a player home"[/i] message means the cell is owned by someone else and isn't tagged as a player house. Just outside a home (its grounds, its cellar) you can add the area to the home instead: walk out of the home and press the hotkey.
[*]In a home with several areas, a chest in another area that shows [i]"only reachable in ..."[/i] in the panel is used only while you are in its own area (the game doesn't keep it in memory elsewhere). Say which area you were in when you report a missing move.
[*][b]VR players:[/b] VR support is experimental, and any report helps, including "it works".
[/list]

[b]Templates:[/b] if you've made a template for a home, post a link here or upload it as its own file. Good ones may be bundled, with credit.
