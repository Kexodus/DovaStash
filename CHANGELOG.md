# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Restock: always carry 10 health potions, 50 steel arrows, 5 lockpicks. Tick *Restock* on a keep quota (Keep rules, for one home or every home; the Dova Stash Editor has it too) and every Deposit tops you back up to that number from the home's containers: the most valuable copies first, from the container they belong in first, never more than the quota. *Restock* is also in the hotkey menu and the panel on its own. HUD: "Dova Stash: deposited 12 items into 3 containers, restocked 10". Quest items, ignored containers and displays are never touched. A new quota starts with Restock ticked; quotas you already have don't restock until you tick it (`"restock": true` in the JSON). Setting: "Restock after Deposit" (on).
- Homes with their own sorting system are left alone, so the two never move the same items: Clockwork, Legacy of the Dragonborn, Mirele Bismath Reborn, LUFTSHIP K747, Skyfall Estate, Riverside Lodge, The Hoarder's Chest - Rebuilt, The Dovah Den, the five city homes with Player houses overhaul, and Tundra Homestead with Tundra Homestead - LotD Storage. With Carpenter Storage Chests for Hearthfire only its building-supply chests are left out; the homes still work. The list is a data file (`SelfSorting/00_Default.json`): a patch, or you, can add a home, some of its cells or single containers by dropping a JSON file next to it, no new DLL needed. `esm_dump.py check` validates it.
- Templates in homes another mod rebuilt: a template can name the mods that rebuild its home (`conflicts`) and is not offered while one is loaded. The Breezehome template steps aside for Wynter's Breezehome Redux, the city homes' for Player houses overhaul. For other mods, a template is enabled on its own only when most of what it names (60%) is still there; otherwise the HUD says "Breezehome setup may not fit this home" and the hotkey menu still offers it ("may not fit"). Hearthfire furniture you haven't built yet counts as there. The editor's Template panel has a Conflicts field.
- A display's Categories only offer what it can hold: a weapon rack or plaque the weapon categories, a shield rack shields, a dagger display daggers, a mannequin the armor it wears, a bookshelf books; never soul gems, potions or misc items. The same in the hotkey menu, the SKSE Menu Framework's Displays page and the Dova Stash Editor, for your own and OCF categories too. A category that can't go on a display, already in a setup or template, stays in the file but puts nothing on it: the editor's Checks and `esm_dump.py check` warn about it, and the log says so once.
- Sell chest: press the hotkey at a container and choose "Make sell chest..." to turn your junk into gold. It takes items like any container (its categories, Clutter to start with, or what you put in by hand), and nothing else is ever taken out of it. Nothing is sold until you choose "Sell now (862 gold)..." in its menu, the master's menu or the panel and confirm the total ("Sell 14 items for 862 gold?", with what goes and what stays). You get what a merchant would pay you (your Speech, Haggling and Fortify Barter). Stolen, improved, enchanted-by-you, renamed, quest and favorited items, potions you made, and what your keep rules always keep (gold, lockpicks, torches) are never sold. Changed your mind? "Buy back" returns your last sale of the session for the same gold. The panel lists this session's sales, and every sale is in the log. Settings: "Price" (100% of the game's) and "Sell by itself after" (off): with it on, what has been in the sell chest that long is sold when you come home, close a container or wake up there. Templates and the Dova Stash Editor can mark a sell chest (`"sell": true`).
- Displays: look at a weapon rack, mannequin, or a plaque made for one item (dragon priest masks, dragon claws, Elder Scrolls...) and press the hotkey to use it, and pick what it takes under Categories, like a container (a rack for bows only, a mannequin for light armor). Deposit and Sort all then keep it filled, as if you had placed it by hand: racks get your best weapons (one of each), coat-of-arms racks a shield, the Unofficial Patch's glass display cases a weapon, mannequins a matching armor set, and a plaque only its own item (written into the home's setup when you turn it on). This includes the Creation Club homes' own mask, claw, Elder Scroll and artifact plaques (Hendraheim, Bloodchill Manor, Myrwatch and others). Weapons go up the way the game's own racks place them, on the same spot (the Unofficial Patch's racks too, with their crossbow, staff and shield spots; what they turn away, such as a staff too long for a display case, stays off), so you take them down as usual; they just appear on the rack, never seen falling or floating on the way. Works in any home from the displays' own scripts; templates can turn displays on and pick their items (`displays`). Nothing is taken off a display. Setting: "Put gear on the home's displays".
- Bookshelves are displays: look at a bookshelf (the vanilla and Hearthfire ones, Creation Club homes' and home mods' copies of them) or a book on it and press the hotkey to use it, and pick what it takes under Categories (skill books, spell tomes, notes...). Deposit and Sort all then put your books on it, as many as it holds and never more, so it never hands any back or says there is no more room: skill books first, then spell tomes, then the most valuable, one copy of each title in the home; never an Elder Scroll, and notes only when you pick them. The books show on the shelf right away, as when you close its menu, and you take them off as usual. Templates can list bookshelves too (`"kind": "bookshelf"`, with categories, preferred `items` and `only`); the shipped ones still keep them out of storage. `esm_dump.py cell` lists bookshelves with how many books each holds, and `check` validates them.
- Materials at the workbench: at a forge, smelter, tanning rack, sharpening wheel, armor table, cooking pot, alchemy lab or any modded station in a home you set up, you craft from what the home's containers hold, as if you carried it: the menu counts the stored materials (`Iron Ingot (12/1)`), the alchemy lab lists your stored ingredients, and each craft takes only what you lack, from the container that item is sorted to first, then the others. Nothing moves when you sit down or get up, so a huge store causes no lag. Enchanters bring your filled soul gems and put the unused ones back. The recipes are read from your load order, so modded materials and recipes work without a patch. Tempered, enchanted, favorited and quest items are never used; weapons and armor that recipes use up (breakdown recipes) only with "Weapons and armor used as materials too". Setting: "Materials at the workbench": Auto (craft from storage), Off, Move (the materials come to you when you sit down and what you didn't use goes back when you leave; what you just made stays with you), or Craft from storage. On VR, Auto means Move. When another mod that crafts from storage is installed (Crafting Pulls Resources From Containers, Skyrim Crafting Inventory Extender, Smart Crafting Storage, Linked Crafting Storage, Workbench Containers), Auto turns ours off and the Settings page and the log say why; choosing a mode yourself overrides that.
- Mannequins: by default each wears your most complete matching armor set, modded armor included: sets are worked out from the plugin, material keyword and piece names ("Valkyrie Cuirass", "Valkyrie Boots"), and for odd mods from what tempers the pieces. To pick one, press the hotkey at a mannequin and open "Armor set... (Auto)": the sets you have, with how much of each, e.g. "Ebony (3/4)". The mannequin then wears only that set and fills up as you get the pieces; nothing is taken off it. The SKSE Menu Framework has an "Armor sets" page: search every armor set in your load order and assign one to a mannequin, even before you own a piece. Layouts store it as `set` (any piece of the set); `esm_dump.py check` validates it.
- SKSE Menu Framework "Displays" page: every rack, mannequin, plaque and display case of the home you are in, with whether it is used, what it takes (categories, armor set, the plaque's item) and what it holds. Filter by kind, used or empty, search, sort. Use or stop using one, pick its categories or armor set (Auto, or any set), Fill now, or "Use all 12 racks" (mannequins, plaques...) in one go, after a confirmation; ones you stopped using stay off. Nothing is taken off a display. The About page says where the Dova Stash Editor is installed, if it is.
- Templates pre-define displays: a template can list exactly which racks, mannequins, plaques and display cases a home uses, and for racks and mannequins what goes on them: categories, preferred `items`, a mannequin's armor `set`, and the new `"only": true` (nothing but those items, which no other display takes, e.g. a rack kept for Dawnbreaker). Items and sets may come from other mods without listing them in `requires`: in a game without the mod they are skipped with one line in the log ("Template My Home: Sword rack: 1 item doesn't exist in this load order (...); skipped") and the rest of the template works. Plaques and cases (dragon priest masks, claws, Elder Scrolls) always keep what the home's own script made them for; a template's items for them are not used. One documented entry shape for layouts and templates, checked by the template lint and `esm_dump.py check` (fields a display doesn't use, `only` without items). Existing layouts and templates load and work exactly as before.
- Homes that span several cells: a cellar, an upper floor or the grounds outside (the forge, smelter and tanning rack of a Hearthfire home) can be part of the same home. Walk out of the home into the next area and press the hotkey: "Add this area to Lakeview Manor". From then on you are in the home there too: the forge outside crafts from the materials stored inside, and Deposit, Sort all and Retrieve use the containers of every area. Containers in another area are used only when the game keeps them in memory (persistent references; most Hearthfire chests, wardrobes, cupboards and safes are), others only from their own area; displays are filled in the area you are in. Outside, the hotkey also works on a container just across the border of the next area while both are loaded. When you add an area you can name it ("Grounds", "Cellar") in the same text box as Rename; the name shows in the prompts, the panel's header and its Areas list, and you can rename it later from the master menu in that area or from the Areas list. The master menu (and the panel's Areas list) removes an area again; no items are moved either way. Templates can list the cells too (optional `cells`, next to `cell`); `esm_dump.py cell` shows which containers the other cells reach (`reachableFromOtherCells`) and `check` validates `cells`. Every existing setup and template, including the separate cellar templates, loads and works exactly as before; a cellar with its own setup stays its own home.
- Remove: the container menu (and the panel) can take a container out of the home's setup, forgetting its categories and learned items; what's inside stays. Ignore still keeps one out for good.
- A container with learned items only names their categories: the activate prompt reads "End Table (Books, learned)", and the category picker and the panel show "Books (1 learned)".
- Save as template...: the hotkey menu (lists and message boxes) saves this home's setup as a template in `Templates/User/`, under a name you type (the home's name to start with), with your character as author. It is offered at once, so after Reset home (or a lost `Layouts` folder) Use template brings the setup back: master, categories, ignored containers, displays, mannequin sets, keep rules and learned items (kept as fixed item bindings, so they go to the same containers; HUD: "incl. 12 learned items"; the panel's Template tab has a "Keep learned items" box to leave them out of a template you share). Saving over the same name asks first, backs up the old file and raises its version (1.0.0, 1.0.1...).
- Setups are backed up outside the Data folder: every time a home's setup changes, a copy goes to `Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE/DovaStash/Backups` (Skyrim VR: `My Games/Skyrim VR`), with copies from your last 5 sessions. MO2 does not put it in its overwrite folder, so cleaning that folder no longer loses your setups. Setups you already have are copied there at the next game start.
- Learning, made easier to live with: the HUD line for a learn offers an undo ("Iron Sword now goes to End Table (K: undo)"): press the hotkey within 5 seconds and the learn is taken back, a container that only entered the setup with it included (setting: "Undo time for a learn"). After three learned items of one category point at the same container, Dova Stash asks once when you close it, "Send all Books to End Table?": Yes binds the category there and drops the learned items it now covers, No is remembered for that container and category (the panel's container page shows the same question, lists the declined ones and can ask again; setting: "Suggest a category after", 0 = never). Enchanted and other variant items count as the item they are made from: an "Iron Sword of Burning" put away by hand teaches where every iron sword goes, and a template's fixed item binding for the Iron Sword takes its enchanted variants too. The panel's Learned tab marks entries that do nothing any more (their container is ignored, gone from the setup, or the master) and forgets them in one go; entries for containers that are merely unbuilt or not loaded are kept and say so.
- Switch templates in the SKSE Menu Framework: This home > Actions now starts with "Templates for this home", every template that fits the home (its plugins loaded), the one offered first at the top, the one in use marked "(in use)" and editor drafts marked "(draft)". Use switches to another in one click; when your setup has changes of your own (categories, master), it asks first and says they are backed up (Saved setups brings them back). Your learned items, keep rules and ignored containers stay, nothing is taken out of a container or off a display, and the next Sort all follows the new setup. The Templates page shows the same list for the home you are in, and marks drafts in its table.
- Restore your last setup: entering a home whose setup file is gone shows "Dova Stash: a saved setup for Whitepeak Tower can be restored (press K)", and the hotkey's setup menu offers "Restore your last setup (2026-10-12 14:03)" first. The panel lists the saved copies under Actions > Saved setups, each with a Restore button (asks first and backs up the current setup); the latest copy is only offered when it differs from the setup as it is now (otherwise the panel says so), older copies always. A broken copy is never used. While a copy can be restored, no template is turned on by itself.

### Fixed

- A plain stack stored next to a container's own stock (a vanilla alchemy sack's salt, a food barrel's bread) could trip the special-copy check: the log said "Move of plain Salt Pile ... took a tempered, enchanted, renamed or worn copy; put 0 back", and from then on that item stayed put in that container for the session (crafting from storage fell back to Move). Lists that only carry a count or an owner now count as plain, as they are.
- Make sell chest binds Clutter, but when the home's template already had a clutter container the chest never received any: list order decided ties. The sell chest now wins a tie for its own categories (a more specific pattern elsewhere still wins).
- Test harness (not shipped): plans declare their test templates, `run.ps1 -Only`, extra home mods for the test profile, cross-cell "nothing lost" checks, message boxes left open are reported, and 60 new plans over 29 homes (city, Hearthfire, Creation Club, mod homes). `esm_dump.py cell` lists furniture (with crafting keywords) and doors (with the cell they lead to).

- Plugins with non-ASCII names (`Château.esp`, `风舞.esp`) keep their names in setups and templates. Before, every form of such a plugin was written as `���.esp|0x...`, so the setup silently did nothing for those containers after the next load (nothing was lost; the entries were kept).
- Deposit put an item away, then took it for a plaque, rack, mannequin or bookshelf in the same run; from then on it treated that item as one "a script hands back" and never put it away again that session. Taking it for a display no longer counts.
- Loading another save mid-session: the Controller forgot nothing of the old game (a container that was open, a deferred sort-on-arrival, the undo window, a "wait until the container menu closes" step), so an action of the old save could run in the new one. A fill still placing items on displays could also keep going with the old game's references. Both are dropped on load and new game; learned destinations not yet written are saved first.
- Fill now on one display took what another display is made for (a plaque's dragon claw, a rack kept for Dawnbreaker). It now plans that display among the others, as Deposit does.
- Armor set... at a mannequin listed sets you were wearing or had favorited as "had" (Ebony (4/4)) although worn and favorited gear never goes up; it counts carried pieces only.
- Unnamed mannequins got a blank name in the set picker's title, the HUD lines and a layout entry written from the panel; they are called "Mannequin".
- A hotkey list could stay open but invisible (input locked until Esc) when an entry that keeps the list open was chosen within the first half second, while the list was still being centred.
- A container or item name ending in `;` dropped its row from the hotkey lists.
- Hand-edited setups and templates: a second entry for the same container or display is kept but never used (the first one wins everywhere; before, the two could both apply and contradict each other); a wrongly typed `name`, `author`, `version` or `priority` is kept as written instead of being replaced by a default on the next save; a template named `con.txt` or another Windows device name gets a `_` so the file can be written; the template check also lists the plugins of a container's fixed items and of keep rules under `requires`.
- A category pack with a `priority` outside the 32-bit range ranked below the defaults instead of above them.
- At the workbench, copies you crafted while only stolen or souled copies of that material were brought stayed with you together with those copies; the crafted copies now count against the plain stack and the brought copies go back.
- `fRadius` and `fIconSize` set to `nan` or `inf` in the INI fall back to the defaults.
- Mannequins without a name (Whitepeak Tower's, many home mods') can be set up: aiming at one and pressing K opens its menu, with its armor set and categories (it opened the master's menu, as the game shows no prompt for them). "Use this rack/mannequin" goes straight on to its options instead of needing another press.
- With "Set up homes from a template on first visit" on, the notice on entering a home says what to do: "Dova Stash: Breezehome is set up. Press K anywhere here to put your loot away." (it used to say only that the setup was enabled).
- Taking a just-deposited item back out through QuickLoot IE's loot menu no longer marks it as "comes back when stored" (an item that would never be moved again that session): only a script handing it back counts, as with the normal container menu.
- Saved templates list the plugins of the displays they use in `requires`, so they pass the template check (not the plugins of display items or mannequin sets: those are optional, see Templates pre-define displays).
- The panel's Template tab starts from the current home's name after you move to another home, instead of the first home's.
- Materials at the workbench, Craft from storage (Skyrim AE): the crafting menu did not count the stored materials and a craft took nothing from the containers, because the menu runs on another game thread than the one the visit started on. The menu now counts and takes them whatever thread the game uses.
- Arrows or bolts picked up after equipping that type stay on you with the rest of the quiver when you Deposit. Before, Deposit took them out of the equipped stack, and from then on left the spare copies of every worn item on you for the rest of the session (nothing was lost). If the game ever takes a special copy for a plain one again, only that item stays put for the session.
- The startup log's Environment block names the in-world menus your `iMode` picks (it always said UIExtensions lists when UIExtensions was installed).

- Spare copies of worn gear are deposited now. Before, a second Iron Shield stayed on you while you wore one. Each such move checks that the worn, tempered or enchanted copy stayed behind.
- Shelves, racks and cupboards that open a hidden chest (an activator linked to it, like Whitepeak Tower's Food shelf or a vanilla bookshelf) can be set up with the hotkey now. They show the chest's categories in the prompt and as floating icons.
- Items that a script hands straight back when stored, such as the Curators Storage Token, stay on you after the first try instead of coming back (and opening their prompt) on every Deposit.
- The hotkey lists and the rename box appear already in the middle of the screen, instead of showing at the skin's position and then jumping there.

### Changed

- The SKSE Menu Framework pages read and behave the same everywhere: one dimmed intro line per page, tooltips that say what a control does (also on greyed-out ones), "..." on every button that asks first, small buttons on list rows, empty states in the same dimmed style, and the actions that reset or remove things at the bottom; Sell now, Buy back and Make sell chest... ask first like every other such button. Settings are grouped into Hotkey, Menus, Sorting, Learning, Crafting, Templates, Sell chest, QuickLoot IE, In the world and Log, with shorter labels; the global keep rules are called "Default keep rules" everywhere. The pages are listed with this home's first (This home, Displays, Armor sets), then Settings, Templates, Diagnostics and About.
- Homes with a template are set up as soon as you walk in: "Set up homes from a template on first visit" (`bAutoEnableTemplates`) is now on by default. Turn it off to get the "setup available" notice instead. An INI you already have keeps its value.
- The hotkey at a bookshelf opens its display menu instead of the container menu; one your setup already stores items in keeps its container menu.
- Plainer words in the game: the HUD, menus and panel say "setup" instead of "layout" ("Breezehome setup enabled", "Custom setup (not from a template)", "this home's setup file is broken"). File names and the template format are unchanged (`Layouts/`, `dovastash-layout-1`).
- The panel's Template tab saves the same way as the hotkey: the template is offered right away (no restart), the author defaults to your character and the version is automatic, and overwriting backs up the old file.
- Your own saved templates come first for their home (ahead of shipped ones of the same priority), so the hotkey and the first-visit notice offer them.
- A template the editor's *Draft all homes* saved (marked `"autoDraft": true`, nobody has saved it since) comes after every template someone made for the same home, whatever its priority or version: the hotkey, the first-visit notice and "Set up homes from a template on first visit" use it only in a home that has no other template. Applying one gives a normal setup.
- The hotkey menu says Re-apply template only for the template the home's setup came from; another template reads Use template.
- `esm_dump.py check` accepts `//` and `/* */` comments in templates, as the game does.
- Templates and saved layouts are easier to read and edit by hand: one line per container with the columns lined up, and short names. The reason a container is left alone is in a separate `note` (shown in the panel under the container's name) instead of filling its name.
- Floating icons show a container's full category row, like QuickLoot's bottom-right row: up to five icons, then "+N". QuickLoot and the overlay now share one icon-row function.
- Every hotkey list starts with a header naming the menu ("-- Whitepeak Tower --", "-- Chest: Categories --"), with the Dova Stash logo above it in your list skin's text colour. Choosing the header does nothing.
- Hotkey lists show category icons from your own SkyUI icons: the category picker, the display picker, Retrieve, Browse containers (each container's icon) and a container's Categories... entry.
- Ticking a category in the hotkey lists updates the list in place, keeping your place in it, instead of closing and reopening the menu.

### Docs

- Dova Stash Editor (in development, `editor/`, design in `docs/EDITOR.md`): a standalone program that reads your MO2 mod list (names, Nexus mod ids, plugins), finds the player homes in a mod's plugins, draws each cell's floor plan with its containers, racks, mannequins, plaques and doors, and drafts a template; preset `editor`. Decided (EDITOR.md, Decisions): objects are picked on the 2D floor plan now and in a Creation Kit style 3D view later; the editor ships inside the main zip; under MO2 it saves templates into a "Dova Stash Templates" mod by default; it loads the whole load order by default (toggle: home plugin + masters only); a home over several cells is one template file.
- Dova Stash Editor: a Creation Kit style 3D view of the home (meshes and textures from your mods and archives, exterior terrain and the cells around it, fly camera, select mode shared with the floor plan), a role editor (master, categories, ignored, displays) and saving into a "Dova Stash Templates" mod; the home list stays beside the home while you go from one to the next; plaques and display cases are the ones Dova Stash fills, read from the home's compiled scripts (a dining table that sets out a meal is no display); a mannequin's armor set is picked from every set of the load order (searchable, as the in-game picker), and "Where items go" fills it with that set; `--render-cell` draws a cell to a PNG without a window. How to try it: `docs/EDITOR.md` §9.
- Dova Stash Editor: a start page with your homes and their to-do stage, "Draft all homes" (every home of your load order with a draft template: the master and each container's categories suggested from its name, the stations and items around it, with a confidence and the reason; a container its author labelled ("Soul Gems", "Dragonpriest Masks") gets only what its label says and is the master only when nothing plain is usable; accept or reject each, then save), a "Where items go" preview that runs Dova Stash's own planner over a sample haul and colours the containers by fill, paint mode, multi-select and undo/redo; `--draft-all` and `--preview` do the same without a window (`docs/EDITOR.md` §3.4-3.7).
- Dova Stash Editor: the home list shows every player home of your load order under the mod that adds it (filter by home, mod name or Nexus id; each home with its storage and template), one click to open, instead of picking a mod and then its cells. The old way stays as *All cells...* under each mod, for a home the finder misses.
- Dova Stash Editor: one window instead of three modes. Your homes stay in a rail on the left (one search box for home, mod, plugin or Nexus id, a filter, each home with its storage and a chip: yours, shipped, auto-draft, draft, none; *All cells...* under a mod for a home the finder misses), the home in 3D or as a plan in the middle with a small floating toolbar (Select, Paint, Layers, Colours, Camera), and on the right the inspector: Selection, Template, Draft and Checks. The wide tables (Containers, Displays, Doors, Where items go) are a drawer under the home; a status row shows the home, errors and warnings, and what is loading. The start page is now a panel in the middle while no home is open.
- Dova Stash Editor: a dark, warm look with Cinzel and Jost (the press kit's fonts, built in), drawn icons, and the selected object's own mesh as a small picture.
- Dova Stash Editor: Ctrl+K opens a command palette: type to find a command, a home, a container of the open home, or (while painting) a category. Every toolbar button has a key (EDITOR-UX.md §4); Ctrl+1/2/3 fold the rail, drawer and inspector, and the window remembers their sizes and the view.
- Dova Stash Editor: a draft is reviewed in the Draft panel of the open home, and it edits the template directly: accepting a suggestion is the category in the Selection panel, the checks and Where items go at once, and undo covers it. Opening a home from the rail starts from its draft, with every cell the draft found.
- Dova Stash Editor: Draft all homes (Ctrl+D) runs in the background with its progress in the status row, then marks each home *drafted* and offers *Save N drafts...* over the rail. It no longer moves every home to "drafted" on the start panel: a home's stage starts when you open it.
- Dova Stash Editor: switching homes keeps each home's unsaved draft (the dot stays on its row); *Save N drafts...* and closing the window cover them.
- Dova Stash Editor: keep rules got an editor in the Template panel (always keep, never keep, quotas, the player's own rules on or off). *Save as...* writes a copy anywhere; Save waits while the checks find an error.
- Dova Stash Editor: the Checks panel lists problems errors first (click one to select the object) and what the template still lacks, such as containers without a role. S in paint mode returns to Select.
- Dova Stash Editor command line: `--home <text>`, `--inspector selection|template|draft|checks`, `--palette`, `--popover <id>` and `--window WxH` for screenshots; `--tab 3d|plan` picks the view, `--tab containers|displays|doors|preview` opens the drawer, `--tab template` the Template panel.
- `docs/EDITOR-UX.md` specifies the editor's one-workspace UI; EDITOR.md §2 and §9, the user guide's text, BUILDING.md and the editor checklist (TESTING.md §8) describe the new window.
- Dova Stash Editor: the window follows the press kit's brand (`presskit/README.md`, Style): black, white and neutral grays only, the wordmark and titles in tracked Cinzel capitals, the emblem as the only ornament, no game texture behind the start panel. Colour is kept for state and data: the pills (drafted, shipped, yours, saved, confidence), the status dots, the viewport's categories, fill and see-through boxes. *Draft all homes* and *Save N drafts...* live in the *Draft* menu only.
- Dova Stash Editor: templates are saved one folder per home, `Templates/<Home>/<Author>.json`, so every template of a home sits together (the game reads `Templates/**`, so nothing changes for it). The Template panel starts with *This home's templates*: every template the home has (shipped, yours, other authors') with author, version and the mod it comes from, the open one marked; click another to open that file. The rail says how many templates a home has.
- Dova Stash Editor: *Save N drafts...* and `--draft-all --save-to` save auto-drafts: each file is marked `"autoDraft": true` and only ever replaces that home's own earlier auto-draft. A template someone made (hand-made, downloaded, or an auto-draft saved since) is never overwritten; the homes left alone are listed by name in the dialog and get a *not saved* line on the command line, as does a second home with the same name. A draft you reviewed first is saved as yours. Open an auto-draft and Save (Ctrl+S) to make it yours: the mark goes and no bulk draft touches it again.
